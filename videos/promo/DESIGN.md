# Brand Visual Identity - Ángel Ruiz

## Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** `#020617` (Deep Dark Navy/Slate)
- **Secondary Background:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
- **Accent/Highlight:** `#F59E0B` (Amber 500 / Gold Accent)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#FBBF24` (Amber 400)
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
- **Text Secondary:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
- **Text Muted:** `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)

## Typography
- **Heading Font:** `Cinzel`, serif (elegant, classical, mysterious feel)
- **Body Font:** `Playfair Display`, serif (sophisticated, premium)
- **UI/Support Font:** `Inter`, sans-serif (clean, modern, legible)

## Component Styles
- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), black background with gold border (`#F59E0B`) or solid amber background. Often carries an amber glow (`box-shadow: rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3) 0px 0px 12px`).
- **Cards/Containers:** Dark semi-transparent slate with backdrop-blur, thin white/slate borders (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Themes:** Dark Mode exclusively, premium luxury magician feel, mystery, elite craft.

## Do's & Don'ts
- **DO:** Use gold/amber gradients and glows to represent magic.
- **DO:** Keep layout clean, spaced, and mysterious with dark backgrounds.
- **DO:** Use elegant serif headings (`Cinzel`) to denote prestige.
- **DON'T:** Use bright primary colors (pure red, green, blue).
- **DON'T:** Use harsh, unrounded borders.
- **DON'T:** Overcrowd the layouts; let elements breathe.
